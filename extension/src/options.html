Audiobookshelf Helper

Server

Used as a Bearer header, and as ?token= on download URLs.
Access to your server
This add-on ships with no site access at all. It needs permission for your own Audiobookshelf origin — and nothing else — to read your library and to put the sync button on its pages.

Device

Absolute path to the mounted player. The helper writes here. Books are fetched from your Audiobookshelf server - nothing else needs to be mounted.
Some players give a named folder resume and bookmarks - check your manual.

Naming

Placeholders: {author} {title} {series}. This also decides how a book is recognised on the device, so changing it makes already-synced books look absent.
